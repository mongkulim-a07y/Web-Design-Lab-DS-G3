About CodeCup

CodeCup was founded by software developers who needed a quiet place with great coffee and strong Wi-Fi to work. What started as a small dream grew into a favorite local community hub for tech enthusiasts, students, and coffee lovers alike. We are passionate about roasting quality beans and providing a warm, productive space for everyone.

Our Core Values:

  • Quality Beans: Handcrafted coffee sourced from ethical farms.
  • Community First: A friendly space where creative minds connect.
  • Productive Vibe: Plenty of power outlets and high-speed internet for studying or coding.
